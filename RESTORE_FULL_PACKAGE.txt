Then rebuild the existing downloadable Gabby gallery bundle:

macOS / Linux:

  ./restore-v2.3.sh

Windows PowerShell:
  cd denzel-tinashe-portfolio-v2.3
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\restore-v2.3.ps1
